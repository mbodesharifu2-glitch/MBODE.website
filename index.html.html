Welcome to St. Mary's Modern High School

Growing minds. Building character. Inspiring the future.

A Community of Learning and Growth

St. Mary's Modern High School is committed to providing a supportive and stimulating environment where learners are encouraged to discover their abilities and reach their full potential.

The school promotes academic achievement, discipline, leadership, practical skills and co-curricular development.

We believe that education should prepare learners not only for examinations but also for responsible and productive participation in society.
